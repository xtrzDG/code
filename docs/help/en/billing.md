# Plan and billing

Everything is in **Settings → [Plan and billing](cabinet:settings/billing)** (owners only).

## The free trial

The trial starts by itself when the assistant goes live, so every trial day is spent with real customers. Nothing is charged during the trial. A few days before it ends the cabinet reminds you to pay.

## Plans and usage

Each plan includes a number of conversations and call minutes a month; the meters show how much is used. Prices are in your country's currency. You can change the plan at any time.

## Payment and invoices

Pay by card on the secure payment page. Invoices appear under **Invoices** when you pay or when the trial ends.

## If a payment fails

The assistant keeps working for a grace period while you pay; the cabinet shows how many days are left. After that it pauses until the payment goes through, and nothing is lost.

## Cancel

You can cancel at any time: the assistant works until the end of the paid period.

> Questions about an invoice or a company's details on it? Write to us in **Help and support**.
