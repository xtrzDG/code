# Inbox

All conversations from every channel are in one place. The assistant answers most of them; the inbox shows the ones that need you.

## Views

- **Needs a person**: the assistant called a person, with the reason and how urgent it is.
- **Requests**: what customers asked for that the assistant cannot do itself (a call back, a group booking, a question for the owner).
- **Mine** and **Unassigned**: who on the team handles what.
- **All**: every conversation, with search and filters.

## Reply

Open a conversation and write in the box at the bottom: your message reaches the customer in the channel they wrote in. Type `/` to insert a quick reply (set them up in **Settings → [Quick replies](cabinet:settings/quick-replies)**). Press **Resolve** when the customer has what they need.

## Work as a team

Assign a conversation to yourself or a colleague from its header. Notes in the side panel are for the team only and never reach the customer or the assistant.

## Never miss one

Turn on notifications in **Settings → [Notifications](cabinet:settings/notifications)**: on this device (push), and for the staff contacts by e-mail, SMS or Telegram, with quiet hours.

> A wrong answer in a conversation? [Teach the assistant](teach-your-assistant) so it answers right next time.
