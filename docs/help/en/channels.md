# Where your customers write and call

Open **Assistant → [Channels](cabinet:assistant/channels)**. Each channel card shows whether it is connected, when it last worked and, if something broke, what to do.

| Channel | What you need | Time |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Website chat | Access to your website, or just the link to your chat page | 2 minutes |
| Telegram | A Telegram account | 2 minutes |
| WhatsApp | A number registered in the WhatsApp Business Platform | a few days for Meta's checks |
| Instagram, Messenger | A Facebook page (and a professional Instagram account) | 15 minutes |
| Phone | A number from us and call forwarding on your phone | 5 minutes |

## A channel shows an error

The card says why: usually a token was revoked or a password changed on the provider's side. Press **Update details**, enter the new details, and the assistant answers there again. Messages that arrived while the channel was down are not lost.

> No website? Share the link to your chat page instead: **Channels → Share** gives the link, a QR code and a printable card for the tables.
