# Telling your staff about transferred calls

When a caller asks for a person, the assistant can put the call through to your staff number while you are open. The people who answer those calls are your employees, so the law asks you to tell them beforehand how the call is handled.

## What happens to a transferred call

- Before the transfer, the caller talks to the assistant; that part is processed like every call (and recorded only if you record calls).
- Once a member of staff answers, **we keep nothing of the staff part of the call**: no recording, no transcript, no summary.
- We keep that a transfer happened, when, to which staff number and whether somebody answered, as part of the call's record, for the retention period you set in **Settings → [Privacy](cabinet:settings/privacy)**.

## Before you switch transfers on

1. Give every person who may take transferred calls the notice below, filled in, and keep a copy with the date they received it.
2. Confirm it in **Settings → [Calls](cabinet:settings/calls)** under "Transfers to staff". We store who confirmed and when; without the confirmation nobody is put through.
3. Give the same notice to anyone who joins later, before they take calls.

## Do not use it to monitor staff

Article 4 of the Italian Workers' Statute (Legge 300/1970) limits tools that can monitor workers remotely. Do not use the assistant's call records, such as unanswered transfers per person, to assess or discipline staff unless you have first met the conditions of article 4 (an agreement with the workers' representatives or an authorisation of the Labour Inspectorate). Recording of transferred calls stays off: the service never records the staff part.

> Have your labour consultant or lawyer check the notice before you use it: it is a starting point, not legal advice.

## Notice template

**Information for staff about calls transferred by the AI assistant (article 13 GDPR)**

[Business name], [address], [tax code or VAT number], as the controller of your personal data, informs you that:

1. Our phone line is answered by an AI assistant run for us by our service provider, which acts as our processor under a data processing agreement. When a caller asks for a person, the call can be put through to the number you gave us: [staff number].
2. Data about you that is processed: your name and the phone number entered in the settings, the time of each transfer and whether it was answered. The conversation between you and the caller after you answer is not recorded, transcribed or summarised.
3. Purpose and legal basis: handling customers' calls, on the basis of our legitimate interest in answering our customers (article 6(1)(f) GDPR) and of your employment contract (article 6(1)(b) GDPR). This data is not used to assess your performance or to monitor your work.
4. Retention: the records of transfers are kept for [retention period] and then deleted.
5. Recipients: our service provider and its sub-processors for telephony, as processors bound by contract. The data is not disclosed to anyone else.
6. Your rights: you can ask for access to your data, its rectification or erasure, the restriction of processing and object to it (articles 15 to 21 GDPR), and you can lodge a complaint with the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali. Contact: [name, e-mail or phone].

Received on [date] — Signature: ____________________
