# Teach your assistant

The assistant answers only from what you told it. When it does not know something, it says so and calls a person; it never invents prices or promises.

## Where its knowledge lives

- **Assistant → [Business profile](cabinet:assistant/profile)**: the place, what you offer, hours and bookings, people, rules. Each card opens the same screens as the setup and saves as you type.
- **Assistant → [Knowledge](cabinet:assistant/knowledge)**: menu items, services, packages, answers to frequent questions and policies. You can import a menu from a photo or PDF, or read it from your website.
- **Knowledge → Questions**: what customers asked and the assistant could not answer. Answer one and it becomes a frequent question.

## Send your changes to customers

Your edits do not reach customers at once. A banner over every page says how many changes wait. Press **Apply changes**: the assistant is rebuilt, checked with a few test conversations and published. If a check fails, the screen shows the conversation that failed and the page that fixes it.

## Fix a wrong answer

1. Open the conversation in the [Inbox](inbox) and read what the customer asked.
2. Add or correct the fact in the profile or knowledge.
3. Ask the same question on the **Try it** screen.
4. Apply changes.

> Advanced: **Updates and checks** lists every published update with its checks; you can go back to an earlier one.
