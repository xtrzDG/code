# Getting started

Your assistant answers customers in the website chat, Telegram, WhatsApp, Instagram, Messenger and on the phone, books visits and calls a person when it should. Here is the shortest way to get it working.

## 1. Create the assistant

Press **Create an AI assistant** and answer eight short questions: what the business is, where it is, what you offer with prices, your hours and booking rules, who to call when a person is needed, and where customers write to you. Everything saves as you type, so you can stop and come back.

## 2. Try it before customers do

On the **Try it** screen write as a customer would: ask for a price, a free time, the address. Nothing reaches real customers. If an answer is wrong, fix the fact in the [business profile or knowledge](teach-your-assistant) and ask again.

## 3. Launch

Press **Launch**. The assistant is checked with test conversations first; if something is missing, the screen says what and opens the page that fixes it. Your free trial starts at the launch, so every trial day is spent with real customers.

## 4. Connect your channels

The website chat works at once. Connect the others in **Assistant → [Channels](cabinet:assistant/channels)**: [Telegram](telegram) takes two minutes, [WhatsApp](whatsapp) and [Instagram and Messenger](instagram-messenger) need a Meta business account, and [call forwarding](call-forwarding) sends the calls you miss to the assistant.

## 5. Invite your team and get notified

In **Settings → [Team](cabinet:settings/team)** invite the people who answer customers. In **Settings → [Notifications](cabinet:settings/notifications)** turn on notifications on your phone, so you know at once when a customer needs a person.

> Stuck? Open **Help and support** in your account menu: write to us in WhatsApp, Telegram or by e-mail and a person will answer.
