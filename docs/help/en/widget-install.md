# Website chat

The website chat is on from the start. Visitors press the button in the corner of your website and talk to the assistant in their language.

## Put it on your website

1. Open **Assistant → [Channels](cabinet:assistant/channels)** and find **Code for your website**.
2. Press **Copy code**.
3. Paste it before the closing `</body>` tag on every page, or into the "custom code" setting of your site builder (WordPress, Tilda, Wix, Shopify and others have one).
4. Open your website and check that the chat button appears.

## Its look

Under **How the chat looks on your website** choose your brand colour and the corner of the button. **Open live preview** shows the chat as visitors see it, before you save.

## No website?

**Channels → Share** gives you the link to your own chat page, a QR code (PNG or SVG) and a printable card for tables, the counter or the door. Put the link in your Instagram profile, Google Maps listing or e-mail signature.

> The code holds no secrets: it is safe to paste anywhere on your site.
