# Bookings

The assistant books only free times by your rules (hours, places or staff, the length of each service, how far ahead) and confirms with the customer in their language.

## The list

**[Bookings](cabinet:bookings)** shows bookings by day with their statuses. On a phone it opens on **Today**: arrivals by time with large **Arrived** and **No-show** buttons.

## Add or change a booking

- **New booking** adds one by hand: choose the service, the free time and the customer.
- Open a booking to confirm, move, edit, cancel or mark it completed. Every change can be undone for a few seconds.
- When you change or cancel a booking, you can send the customer a message right away.

## Reminders and the calendar

- Customers get a reminder before their visit in the channel they booked in.
- Connect **Google Calendar** in **Assistant → [Channels](cabinet:assistant/channels)** to see the bookings in your calendar too.

## Change the rules

Hours, places and booking rules are in **Assistant → [Business profile](cabinet:assistant/profile)** (Hours and bookings). Apply the changes so customers get them.
