# Instagram and Messenger

Both channels connect through your Facebook page.

## Instagram

1. In the Instagram app switch the account to a **professional account** (business or creator) and link it to your Facebook page.
2. Get the **page ID** and a **page access token** with Facebook Login.
3. In **Assistant → [Channels](cabinet:assistant/channels)** press **Connect** on the Instagram card and paste them. The assistant answers direct messages of the Instagram account linked to that page.

## Messenger

1. In your Facebook page's settings copy the **page ID**.
2. Get a **page access token** with Facebook Login, with permission to manage messages.
3. Press **Connect** on the Messenger card and paste them.

## When it stops answering

A page access token stops working when someone changes the Facebook password, removes the app's access or leaves the page's admins. The card then shows an error; get a new token and press **Update details**. The cabinet warns you ahead of time when a token is about to expire.
