# Accessibility text for your terms

Your customers reach you through pages we run for you: the website chat, the chat page, the booking page and its links. The law on accessibility (the European Accessibility Act, in Italy D.Lgs. 82/2022) asks whoever offers these services to say how accessible they are. We publish the statement for our pages; the chat and booking pages link it in their footer ("Accessibility"). Below is a text you can copy.

## Do you need it?

Very small businesses (fewer than 10 people and up to 2 million euros a year) that provide services are exempt from the Act's service duties, but saying how a customer gets help costs nothing and avoids complaints. A lawyer can tell you what applies to your business.

## Text to copy

Replace the parts in square brackets.

> **Accessibility of our online booking and chat.** Our website chat, chat page and booking page are provided by our technology supplier, which publishes an accessibility statement for them, following the AgID model: [link to the "Accessibility" page shown in the footer of our chat or booking page]. The pages aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA and are partially compliant; the statement lists what is not yet accessible. If you cannot use them, you can always book or ask us the same things by phone at [phone number] or by e-mail at [e-mail address], and we will help you in the way that suits you. Tell us about any barrier: we will answer within 30 days. If you are not satisfied with the answer, you can report it to AgID (the Agency for Digital Italy) with its online form at form.agid.gov.it.

## What you should also do

- Keep a phone number or e-mail where customers can book without the online pages, and put it in the text above.
- Answer accessibility requests within 30 days; forward questions about the pages themselves to us through support.
- Photos, menus and texts you upload are your content: give photos a short description and write menus as text, not only as images.
