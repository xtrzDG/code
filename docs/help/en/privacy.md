# Privacy and your customers' data

You decide why customer data is collected; we process it for you under the data processing agreement (DPA).

## The agreement

Read and accept the DPA in **Settings → [Privacy](cabinet:settings/privacy)**. The assistant goes live only after an owner accepts it. The text is in English; the Italian version follows once a lawyer has reviewed it.

## A customer asks for their data

1. Open **Settings → [Privacy](cabinet:settings/privacy)** and find the customer by name or phone.
2. **Export** gives everything stored about them in one file.
3. **Erase** removes their conversations, contact details and recordings for good.

## Leaving or switching to another provider

**Settings → [Privacy](cabinet:settings/privacy) → Export your data → Prepare full export** gives everything in one archive: customers, conversations, calls and their recordings, bookings, the knowledge base, your setup (every version of the assistant's instructions, the call and privacy settings, the channels without their passwords) and your customers' marketing consents. Each kind of record is a JSON file, the cabinet's tables are CSV, and the README inside explains the format. Recordings beyond 2 GB of audio are listed with a link to download them one by one. After you close the business the export stays available for 30 days; then the data is deleted and you get a deletion certificate.

## Who saw what

**Settings → [Audit log](cabinet:settings/audit)** lists every view and change of personal data: who, what and when. When platform support looks into your cabinet at your request, that is listed too, and you see a banner the whole time.

## Good to know

- The assistant says it is an AI in the customer's language at the start of a conversation.
- Call recordings are kept only as long as the retention period allows and are encrypted.
- Your customers' data is stored in the EU.
