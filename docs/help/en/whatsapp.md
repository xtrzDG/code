# WhatsApp

WhatsApp works through Meta's WhatsApp Business Platform. The number you connect is used by the assistant; it cannot stay in the regular WhatsApp app on a phone at the same time.

## Connect

1. In **WhatsApp Manager** register your business number in the WhatsApp Business Platform (Cloud API). Meta checks the business, which can take a few days.
2. Add our business portfolio as a partner of your WhatsApp Business account (**Business settings → WhatsApp accounts → Partners**). Ask us for the portfolio ID in **Help and support**.
3. In WhatsApp Manager open **API setup** and copy the **phone number ID** and the **WhatsApp Business Account ID**.
4. In the cabinet open **Assistant → [Channels](cabinet:assistant/channels)**, press **Connect** on the WhatsApp card and paste both.

We are glad to do this with you on a call: write to us in **Help and support**.

## The 24-hour rule

WhatsApp lets a business write freely only within 24 hours after the customer's last message. Later, only templates approved by Meta can be sent. So staff replies after 24 hours work:

1. Create a template with one variable `{{1}}` in WhatsApp Manager (for example "Reply from our team: {{1}}") and wait for approval.
2. On the WhatsApp card enter its name and language under **Replies after 24 hours**.

The assistant itself always answers within the 24 hours, because it answers at once.
