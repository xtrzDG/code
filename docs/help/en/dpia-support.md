# DPIA support: assessing the assistant's risks

Your business is the controller of its customers' data; we are your processor. Under Article 35 of the GDPR, deciding whether a data protection impact assessment (DPIA) is needed, and writing it, is your task. This page gives you what you need from our side.

## Do you need one?

The Italian supervisory authority (Garante) lists the processing that always needs a DPIA (provvedimento n. 467 of 11 October 2018). Its item 8 covers innovative technologies such as artificial intelligence and voice assistants when another risk factor comes with them. With the assistant, write a DPIA when at least one of these is true:

- you record calls (**Settings → [Calls](cabinet:settings/calls)**);
- customers tell the assistant about health: allergies, intolerances, treatments, pregnancy;
- you serve many customers, for example several locations or a large hotel;
- you send marketing messages to the customers the assistant collected.

When none of these applies, keep a short note of why you decided no DPIA is needed, with the date.

## What we give you

| Your DPIA asks for | Where you find it |
| --- | --- |
| Description of the processing | the data processing agreement (DPA), sections 2 and 3, in **Settings → [Privacy](cabinet:settings/privacy)** |
| Sub-processors and where data goes | DPA section 8, also on the public page of the DPA |
| Security measures | DPA section 9: each measure as the service applies it today |
| Retention | DPA section 13 and your own periods in **Settings → [Privacy](cabinet:settings/privacy)** |
| Data subjects' rights | export and erasure of a customer in **Settings → [Privacy](cabinet:settings/privacy)** ([privacy](privacy)) |
| Who accessed what | **Settings → [Audit log](cabinet:settings/audit)** |
| Breaches | DPA section 12: we tell every owner within 48 hours |

## Risks and how to reduce them

| Risk | What we do | What you can do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The assistant gives wrong information (prices, allergens) | answers only from your facts; replies are checked before they are sent | keep your knowledge up to date; review the questions it could not answer |
| Health details in booking notes | they stay in your business's data, encrypted backups only, never used to train models | tell the assistant not to ask about health; set a shorter retention |
| Call recordings | off until you turn them on; callers hear a notice; encrypted with your business's key | keep them off unless you need them; choose a short retention |
| Data outside the EU | the model and the voice run in the EU; other providers only with safeguards (DPA section 7) | leave channels you do not need switched off |
| Quality review of a sample of conversations | phone numbers and e-mail addresses removed first | turn it off in **Settings → [Privacy](cabinet:settings/privacy)** |
| A staff account is taken over | sign-in codes with limits, two-factor sign-in, a list of signed-in devices | require two factors for your team in **Settings → [Team](cabinet:settings/team)** |

## What stays with you

- A privacy notice on your website and channels that names the assistant and us as your processor.
- A legal basis for any health information your customers volunteer.
- Telling your staff how to use the cabinet and the customers' data.
- Reviewing the DPIA when you change channels, turn recording on or open a new location.

> Questions while you write it? Write to support from the cabinet: helping with impact assessments is part of our duties as your processor (DPA section 6).
