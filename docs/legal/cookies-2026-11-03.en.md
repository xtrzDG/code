## 1. Technical cookies, and one with your consent

The cabinet and our website use first-party cookies only. Most of them are
technical: they make the service work or remember a choice you made, and
they need no consent. One cookie, aw_attr, only helps us understand where
the businesses who sign up come from: we set it only if you accept it on
the banner of our website. We do not use advertising cookies, third-party
analytics or cross-site tracking.

## 2. The cookies

| Cookie | What it does | How long |
| --- | --- | --- |
| __Host-aw_session | Technical. Keeps you signed in. It holds a random token; only its hash is stored on our side. | Until you sign out, at most 30 days |
| aw_locale | Technical. Remembers the language of the cabinet. | 1 year |
| aw_theme | Technical. Remembers the light or dark appearance you chose. | 1 year |
| aw_sidebar | Technical. Remembers whether the side menu is collapsed. | 1 year |
| aw_motion | Technical. Remembers that you asked the cabinet for less motion ("Reduce motion" in the account menu). | 1 year |
| aw_tz | Technical. Remembers your device's time zone, so pages that belong to no business show the times your clock shows. | 1 year |
| aw_consent | Technical. Remembers your answer on the cookie banner (accepted or rejected), so we do not ask again on every page. | 6 months |
| aw_attr | Only with your consent. Remembers which page or link first brought you to our website, so a new account knows where its owner came from. It is first-party only, never shared, and deleted when you sign in or withdraw your consent. | Until you sign in, at most 90 days |

## 3. Your choice

The first time you open our website, a banner asks whether you accept the
aw_attr cookie. "Accept" and "Reject" are offered side by side in the same
way; closing the banner is the same as rejecting it, and until you answer
the cookie is not set. Your answer is kept for 6 months in the aw_consent
cookie; after that, or if the cookies we ask about change, the banner asks
again. You can change your answer at any time with "Cookie settings" at the
foot of every page of our website: withdrawing your consent deletes aw_attr
at once.

The cabinet, the businesses' chat pages, their website chat and the
booking pages show no banner: they set no cookie that needs consent.

## 4. Storage in your browser

The cabinet may keep small settings in your browser's local storage (for
example which hints you have closed), and the assistant's test chat in the
storage of the browser tab, which ends with the tab.

The businesses' chat pages and their website chat set no cookies. A
business's chat page keeps the conversation (a random visitor key, the
latest messages, how far the conversation has been read and whether it was
passed to staff) in the storage of the browser tab only, so it is gone when
the tab is closed; when the page opens, it removes a conversation that an
earlier version of the page kept in the browser's local storage. Only the
language you chose and the answers you rated stay in local storage. The
website chat on a business's own website keeps the same conversation in
that website's local storage, so it continues on the next page or visit,
for up to 24 hours after the last message: a conversation idle for longer
is removed the next time the chat loads. Both keep, for the tab only,
whether the chat is open and the source the page names (for example a QR
code), which the business's reports count.

The booking page opened from a booking confirmation exchanges the link for
an access to the booking of about 30 minutes and keeps it in the storage
of the browser tab only, so reloading the tab still opens the booking; the
link in the confirmation keeps working.

When you sign out, the cabinet asks your browser to delete everything this
site keeps on that device: its cookies (your cookie choice included), its
storage and its cache.

## 5. The bot check

When the sign-in page asks for a bot check, Cloudflare Turnstile runs it in a
frame of its own and handles what it needs under Cloudflare's own privacy
policy. It is not used to track you.

## 6. Deleting cookies

You can delete cookies in your browser at any time. Without the session
cookie you are signed out; without aw_consent the banner asks again; without
the others the cabinet falls back to its defaults.
