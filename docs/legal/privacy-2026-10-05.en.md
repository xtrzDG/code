## 2. What this policy covers

This policy covers the people who use the cabinet (business owners and their
staff) and the visitors of our website. It does not cover the customers who
write to or call a business's assistant: for their data the business is the
controller and we process it on its behalf under the data processing agreement
(DPA); the business's own privacy notice tells them how it is handled.

## 3. What we collect

- **Account:** your phone number or e-mail address, your name if you give it,
  your language and country, the businesses you belong to and your role.
- **Sign-in and security:** one-time codes (only a hash, for at most a day),
  your sessions with their device (browser) and IP address, authenticator
  settings and recovery codes (only hashes), and the security log of your
  sign-ins and sensitive actions.
- **Use of the cabinet:** the steps you take to set up and run your assistant,
  page performance measurements without personal content, and where you first
  came from (the address or campaign that brought you, kept with a new
  account).
- **Notifications:** the addresses, Telegram chats and devices where you
  chose to receive notifications, and your quiet hours.
- **Billing:** the business's legal name, tax number and address, invoices and
  payment status. Card details are handled by the payment provider; we never
  see them.
- **Support:** what you write to us and our answers.

## 4. Why we use it and on what legal basis

- To provide the service you signed up for: your account, sign-in,
  notifications, billing (performance of a contract).
- To keep the service and your account secure: limits on sign-in attempts,
  bot checks, the security log, fraud prevention (legitimate interests and
  legal obligations).
- To improve the product: aggregated measurements of how the cabinet is used
  and how fast it is (legitimate interests).
- To keep invoices and accounting records (legal obligation).
- To tell you about changes to the service, its terms and its sub-processors
  (performance of a contract and legal obligation).

We do not sell your data, do not use it for advertising and do not use it to
train AI models.

## 5. Who receives it

Our sub-processors (hosting, e-mail and SMS delivery, the bot check, payments,
error monitoring) receive only what they need to do their job. The full list
with purposes and locations is in section 8 of the DPA and at
/v1/legal/subprocessors; we announce changes in advance. We disclose data to
authorities only where the law requires it.

## 7. How long we keep it

- Account data: while your account exists, and then as long as needed for the
  purposes above or the law. [Retention after the account is closed.]

## 8. Your rights

You can ask for access to your data, its correction or deletion, the
restriction of its processing, a copy in a portable format, and you can object
to processing based on legitimate interests. Write to [e-mail for privacy
requests]; we answer within one month. You can also complain to your data
protection authority (in Georgia, the Personal Data Protection Service; in the
EU, the authority of your country).

## 9. Security

Traffic is encrypted (TLS), secrets are stored encrypted, each business's data
is separated in the database, sign-in uses one-time codes with an optional
authenticator, and access to personal data is recorded in an audit log.

## 10. Cookies

The cabinet uses only the cookies it needs to work and to remember your
choices. They are listed in the cookie statement.
