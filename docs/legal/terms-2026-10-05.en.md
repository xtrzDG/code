2.1. The Service is an AI assistant that answers the Client's customers on the

or removed. The Provider tells the Client in advance about a change that
removes a feature the Client relies on.

3.2. An owner decides who joins the Client's team and with which role (owner

4.1. The Client is responsible for what it tells the assistant (knowledge,

4.2. The Client uses the Service lawfully, including the rules on personal data,

- tells its customers that they talk to an AI assistant and how their data is

Client's staff when it cannot help, but the Client remains responsible for its
offers and checks important details with its customers.

5.2. The Provider does not use the Client's data or its customers' messages to
train AI models.
