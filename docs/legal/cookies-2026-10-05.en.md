## 1. Only functional cookies

The cabinet and our website use only first-party cookies that make them work
or remember a choice you made. We do not use advertising cookies, third-party
analytics or cross-site tracking, so there is nothing to opt in to.

## 2. The cookies

| Cookie | What it does | How long |
| --- | --- | --- |
| __Host-aw_session | Keeps you signed in. It holds a random token; only its hash is stored on our side. | Until you sign out, at most 30 days |
| aw_locale | Remembers the language of the cabinet. | 1 year |
| aw_theme | Remembers the light or dark appearance you chose. | 1 year |
| aw_sidebar | Remembers whether the side menu is collapsed. | 1 year |
| aw_attr | Remembers which page or link first brought a visitor, so a new account knows where its owner came from. It is first-party only, never shared, and deleted when you sign in. [Confirm whether this cookie needs consent in your clients' countries.] | Until you sign in, at most 90 days |

## 3. Storage in your browser

The cabinet may keep small settings in your browser's local storage (for
example which hints you have closed). The website chat widget and the hosted
chat page set no cookies: they keep a random key in the browser's storage so
a conversation continues where the visitor left it.

## 4. The bot check

When the sign-in page asks for a bot check, Cloudflare Turnstile runs it in a
frame of its own and handles what it needs under Cloudflare's own privacy
policy. It is not used to track you.

## 5. Your choices

You can delete cookies in your browser at any time. Without the session
cookie you are signed out; without the others the cabinet falls back to its
defaults.
